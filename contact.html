What CasePath supports — and what it does not

We are here to help you use CasePath confidently. To set clear expectations:

  • We can help with: account access, billing questions about CasePath plans, technical issues, and feedback on platform workflows.
  • We do not provide: legal advice, representation, or opinions on your case outcome.
  • We do not act on your behalf with courts, lawyers, or the other party.
  • For legal advice, contact a qualified family lawyer or Legal Aid in your state or territory.
