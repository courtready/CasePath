Family law matters can be emotionally demanding and administratively complex. CasePath is built to help people — whether self-represented or working with a lawyer — organise evidence, prepare documents, build chronologies, and approach court processes in a more structured, informed way — with export-ready materials you can discuss with a lawyer.

Better preparation helps you arrive at appointments with clearer information and fewer gaps. CasePath is designed to make workflow and preparation tools more accessible for people navigating Australian family law — not to replace advice, but to support better organisation and preparation discipline.

Built for structured preparation — not legal shortcuts.

Organised preparation can reduce duplicated administrative work and help you make better use of professional legal advice. CasePath is designed to support serious preparation — chronology building, evidence management, and document assistance you can use before, during, and alongside legal advice. It does not predict outcomes and is not a substitute for representation where you need it.
