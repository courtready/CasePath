Family law matters can become costly and emotionally demanding. CasePath is built to help self-represented litigants organise evidence, prepare documents, build chronologies, and approach court processes in a more structured, informed way — with export-ready materials you can discuss with a lawyer.

Even a modest amount of traditional legal assistance can represent a significant expense. CasePath is designed to make workflow and preparation tools more accessible for people navigating Australian family law — not to replace advice, but to support better organisation and drafting discipline.

1. Parties and engagement

This Parenting Pack agreement (the Agreement) is entered into between [Provider name] (we, us, our) and [Client name] (you, your) on the effective date above. By signing, you engage us to provide the Parenting Pack services described below.

2. Services included

The Parenting Pack consists of four components:

2.1 Parenting Orders
Advice, drafting and filing assistance in relation to parenting arrangements under the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth), including initial consultation, drafting of proposed parenting orders or parenting plans, preparation and filing of an Application for Consent Orders (or related court documents) in the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia, correspondence with the other party, and one review of any orders made during the term.

2.2 Output Workflows
A guided chronology-informed drafting service for supporting materials: preparation of affidavits, parenting plans and supporting statements; review and editing of documents you prepare; standard correspondence templates; and document checks before filing or service. Excludes contested litigation, court appearances, or matters outside parenting unless added by separate written scope.

2.3 Your Case
A secure online workspace where you can access and manage your matter for the duration of the Term: a real-time view of case status, key dates and next steps; access to your documents, court filings and correspondence; secure messaging with your legal team; a task list; and the ability to upload material directly into your file.

2.4 Supporting Evidence Workspace
AES-256 — A dedicated, encrypted evidence workspace within Your Case for sensitive evidentiary material. Encryption at rest using AES-256; encryption in transit using TLS 1.2+; access restricted to you and authorised members of your legal team; tamper-evident audit trail for every upload, view and download; original-file preservation; and mandatory two-factor authentication. Encryption protects your data against unauthorised access; it does not guarantee admissibility, which depends on the rules of evidence and the court's discretion.

3. Term

This Agreement commences on the effective date and continues for twelve (12) months (the Initial Term), unless terminated earlier in accordance with clause 8 or rolled over to a Monthly Plan in accordance with clause 4.

4. Monthly Plan rollover

At the end of the Initial Term you may elect to convert to a Monthly Plan that continues full access to all four components on a month-to-month basis.

Month 12 — Choose your path. Convert to Monthly, renew for another 12 months, or end the engagement.

Month 12 + — Monthly Plan. Full read/write access continues. Cancel any time on 30 days' notice.

4.1 What's included

The Monthly Plan provides full read/write access to Your Case and supporting evidence workspace, ongoing Output Workflows at a [reduced/standard] rate or fair-use cap of [N] tasks per month, and ad-hoc Parenting Orders advice at the hourly rate per the current fee schedule.

4.2 Fee and billing

The Monthly Plan fee is [$ amount] per month (incl. GST), billed monthly in advance by direct debit or card on file. The fee may be adjusted on 30 days' written notice; you may cancel without penalty if you do not accept the change.

4.3 How to elect

We will contact you 45 days before the end of the Initial Term with a Monthly Plan election notice. To convert, you must confirm your election in writing (or via Your Case) before the Initial Term ends. The Monthly Plan does not start automatically — if we do not receive your election, your engagement will move to the Retention Period under clause 7.

4.4 Cancellation and pause

You may cancel the Monthly Plan at any time on 30 days' written notice; fees for the notice period remain payable. You may request a pause of up to [X] months once per 12-month period; access becomes read-only during a pause and a reduced [$ amount] per month archive fee applies.

4.5 Reverting to retention

If the Monthly Plan ends — by cancellation, non-payment, or our termination — the Retention Period under clause 7 begins from the date access ends. The 12-month retention clock starts fresh from that date.

5. Fees and payment

Fees for the Initial Term are [$ amount] (incl. GST), payable as set out in Schedule A. Monthly Plan fees are as set out in clause 4.2. Disbursements (court filing fees, expert reports, etc.) are charged at cost in either model.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and complete information, including any safety concerns
  • Provide documents and instructions in a timely manner
  • Notify us promptly of changes in circumstances
  • Keep your Your Case and Evidence Vault credentials secure, enable two-factor authentication, and not share them with third parties
  • Only upload material you are lawfully entitled to hold
  • Keep your billing details current if on the Monthly Plan
  • Not use the services to pursue any unlawful or harassing conduct

7. Confidentiality, privacy and data retention

We handle your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and our Privacy Policy. Communications, including those made through Your Case and material held in the Evidence Vault, are subject to legal professional privilege where applicable. Evidence Vault data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2+.

We will retain your file and personal information — including data and documents stored within Your Case and the Evidence Vault — for a period of twelve (12) months after your engagement ends (the Retention Period). Your engagement ends at the conclusion of the Initial Term or, if you have rolled over to a Monthly Plan, at the end of the final paid month.

During the Retention Period: Your Case becomes read-only; the Vault remains encrypted and accessible for download only; you may request copies of your file at any time, free of charge for the first request. At the end of the Retention Period we will securely destroy or de-identify your personal information, including any data held within the Vault, except where required by law to retain specific records (for example, trust account records under the relevant Legal Profession Uniform Law). Vault destruction is performed via cryptographic key erasure and verified deletion. We will let you know in writing 30 days before destruction so you can request a final copy.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement on 14 days' written notice during the Initial Term, or 30 days' written notice on the Monthly Plan. We may terminate immediately if you fail to pay fees, instruct us to act unethically, or where a conflict of interest arises. On termination, fees are payable for work completed up to the date of termination, your access to Your Case and the Evidence Vault will move to read-only mode, and the data retention obligations in clause 7 still apply.

9. Limitation of liability and dispute resolution

Our liability for any loss arising under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by you in the 12 months preceding the claim, except where liability cannot be limited by law (including under the Australian Consumer Law). If a dispute arises, the parties will first attempt resolution by good-faith discussion; if unresolved within 21 days, the dispute will be referred to mediation before any litigation. This Agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales.
