7. Confidentiality, privacy and data retention

We handle your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and our Privacy Policy. Communications, including those made through Your Case and material held in the Evidence Vault, are subject to legal professional privilege where applicable. Evidence Vault data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2+.

We will retain your file and personal information — including data and documents stored within Your Case and the Evidence Vault — for a period of twelve (12) months after your engagement ends (the Retention Period). Your engagement ends at the conclusion of the Initial Term or, if you have rolled over to a Monthly Plan, at the end of the final paid month.

During the Retention Period: Your Case becomes read-only; the Vault remains encrypted and accessible for download only; you may request copies of your file at any time, free of charge for the first request. At the end of the Retention Period we will securely destroy or de-identify your personal information, including any data held within the Vault, except where required by law to retain specific records (for example, trust account records under the relevant Legal Profession Uniform Law). Vault destruction is performed via cryptographic key erasure and verified deletion. We will let you know in writing 30 days before destruction so you can request a final copy.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement on 14 days' written notice during the Initial Term, or 30 days' written notice on the Monthly Plan. We may terminate immediately if you fail to pay fees, instruct us to act unethically, or where a conflict of interest arises. On termination, fees are payable for work completed up to the date of termination, your access to Your Case and the Evidence Vault will move to read-only mode, and the data retention obligations in clause 7 still apply.

9. Limitation of liability and dispute resolution

Our liability for any loss arising under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by you in the 12 months preceding the claim, except where liability cannot be limited by law (including under the Australian Consumer Law). If a dispute arises, the parties will first attempt resolution by good-faith discussion; if unresolved within 21 days, the dispute will be referred to mediation before any litigation.
