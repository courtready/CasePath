Why We Exist

Our Mission

Helping every Australian get into — and out of — separation with clarity, dignity, and the information they need.

Separation shouldn't cost you everything

Separation and divorce are stressful in every situation: fear of the unknown, not knowing how to move forward, and plain confusion. Every year, tens of thousands of Australians enter the family law system. Most of them have never seen the inside of a courtroom. They don't know which form to file, what a directions hearing is, or what the court means by "the best interests of the child." They are scared, overwhelmed, and often completely alone. The first thing most people think they need to do is go straight to a lawyer.

The legal system was not designed for everyday Australians. It was designed for lawyers — written in language only lawyers understand, structured around processes only lawyers are trained to navigate, and priced at rates only some can afford. The result is a two-tier system: those who can pay for representation, and those who don't qualify for Legal Aid, who are left to figure it out themselves.

CasePath exists to close that gap, through information that is in plain language and offers help with forms and information without the cost of lawyers, especially in the beginning confusing time of separation.

Getting in

The beginning of separation is the hardest part. You don't know what you don't know. You're googling legal terms at midnight, trying to work out whether you need an initiating application or a response, whether you're the applicant or the respondent, and whether you've already missed a deadline you didn't know existed.

CasePath meets you at that moment. We give you a plain-English glossary of every term the court will use. We show you the forms you need, in the order you need them. We walk you through each stage of proceedings — from the first mention to the final hearing — so that when you walk into court, you understand what is happening and why.

We don't replace lawyers. We make sure that when you do speak to one, you understand what they're telling you — and when you can't afford one, you're not left in the dark.

Getting through

Family law cases are not quick. The average contested parenting matter takes 12 to 18 months. During that time, you are expected to file documents, meet deadlines, attend hearings, manage evidence, co-parent under pressure, and somehow keep your life together. The emotional toll is immense — and it is rarely acknowledged by the system itself.

CasePath gives you Your Case to track every phase, a secure vault for your evidence, a document helper that turns your rough notes into court-ready filings, and mental health resources that treat your wellbeing as central to your case — not a footnote. We help you to stay organised, stay informed, and stay standing.

Getting out

The goal of every family law matter should be resolution — not indefinite conflict. Whether that means consent orders, a negotiated property settlement, or final orders after a hearing, the end of proceedings should be the beginning of a new chapter. For you, and especially for your children.

CasePath is built to help you reach that point. We encourage early resolution where possible, help you understand what a realistic outcome looks like, and give you the tools to move forward — not just as a litigant, but as a parent, a person, and a human being who has been through something genuinely hard. Regardless of whether children are involved or not.

Protecting the children

The full About the Kids guide lives in the main CasePath experience.

Children did not choose this. They did not file the application. They did not create the conflict. But they absorb its consequences — every overheard argument, every late changeover, every month the proceedings drag on. The longer a case takes, and the more adversarial it becomes, the more damage is done to the people who matter most.

The research is clear: where it is safe, children do best when they can have a meaningful relationship with both parents. Australian law centres each decision on the child's best interests, with protection from harm as the paramount consideration, then a structured list of factors the court must weigh (Family Law Act 1975 (Cth) s 60CC). That framework is not a slogan — it is the standard every parenting decision must meet.

Yet too often, the family court process itself becomes the source of harm. Children are caught between warring parents for months or years. They lose time with a parent not because of any finding of risk, but because proceedings are slow, expensive, and adversarial by design. By the time a final order is made, the damage to the parent-child relationship may already be done.

CasePath is built with a simple principle: get parents to resolution as quickly as possible, preserve both relationships wherever it is safe to do so, and never lose sight of the fact that behind every case number is a child who needs stability, love, and both of their parents.

What we believe

⚖️

Access to justice is not a luxury. Every person deserves to understand the legal process that is reshaping their family, regardless of whether they can afford a lawyer.

🧠

Mental health is not a side issue. The emotional weight of separation affects every decision you make — in court and out of it. Supporting your wellbeing is part of supporting your case.

👶

Children come first. Every parenting decision the court makes — and every tool we build — is grounded in the best interests of the child. Children need stability, routine, and the knowledge that both of their parents love them and are present. That is not a slogan. It is the law, and it should be the practice.

👨‍👩‍👧

Both parents matter. Where it is safe and in a child's best interests, arrangements should support a meaningful, ongoing relationship with each parent. Children thrive when conflict is low and they are free to love both parents without being put in the middle. The court process should protect those relationships, not erode them through delay and unnecessary conflict.

🛡️

Safety is non-negotiable. Meaningful time with both parents is a goal only where it is safe. Where there is family violence, abuse, neglect, or genuine risk to a child, the court must act to protect them. CasePath provides information about safety orders, supervised contact, and protective measures because keeping children safe always comes first.

🤝

Resolution is the goal. We do not help people fight harder. We help people understand their situation clearly so they can reach the best possible outcome — ideally by agreement, as quickly as possible, and in a way that minimises the harm done to children through the family court process.

🔒

Your information is yours. Everything you add to The Vault on CasePath is encrypted, private, and under your control. We do not sell data, serve ads, or share your information with anyone — ever.
