A Simple Process

Most people arrive at CasePath feeling overwhelmed.

Court documents, deadlines, parenting arrangements, mediation, evidence and legal terminology can quickly become difficult to manage.

CasePath is designed to help you organise everything in one place and work through preparation step by step.

Prefer a shorter path with links into these steps? Use the Quick Start Guide in the Documentation Centre.

  1. 1

    Create Your Account

    Create an account and access your private case workspace.
  2. 2

    Build Your Profile

    Add your matter, court information, participants, children and important dates.
  3. 3

    Prepare

    Use checklists, guides and preparation tools tailored to your situation.
  4. 4

    Create Documents

    Prepare structured worksheets and draft material for review.
  5. 5

    Stay Organised

    Track dates, documents, deadlines and progress throughout your matter.

Step 1 — Create Your Account

Creating an account gives you access to your private CasePath workspace.

Your information remains connected to your matter so you can return at any time and continue where you left off.

Your private workspace

Sign in from any device to pick up your preparation, documents and case notes where you left them.

Step 2 — Build Your Case Profile

Your profile becomes the foundation for everything else inside CasePath.

You can store information about:

  • Court details
  • Children
  • Other parties
  • Important dates
  • Existing orders
  • Support people
  • Key case information

Information you enter once can be reused across preparation tools and document assistants, so you are not retyping the same details each time.

Court details

Registry, file numbers, and hearing information kept in one place.

Children & parties

Names, relationships and contact details for everyone involved in your matter.

Important dates

Hearings, deadlines, mediation sessions and handover times recorded as you go.

Orders & case notes

Existing orders, key facts and support contacts linked to your matter.

Step 3 — Prepare With Guided Tools

CasePath includes practical tools designed to help you prepare for common family law and protection order situations.

Parenting Orders Preparation

Work through parenting arrangement questions, handover logistics and order-related checklists in a structured way.

Mediation Preparation

Organise issues, concerns and proposals before a mediation session so you know what you want to discuss.

Court Appearance Checklists

Step-by-step lists for hearings, conferences and filing deadlines so nothing is missed on the day.

Family Report Preparation

Prepare for interviews and gather relevant background information about your children and their circumstances.

Evidence Organisation

Sort documents, messages and records by date and relevance so you can find what you need when you need it.

Safety Planning

Tools for protection order matters and immediate safety considerations when family violence is involved.

Step 4 — Create Documents Faster

CasePath includes guided document assistants that help organise information into a structured format.

Examples include:

  • Parenting Orders Worksheet
  • Affidavit & Statement Preparation
  • Court Application Worksheets
Important: CasePath helps organise information. It does not provide legal advice or tell you what outcome to seek.

Step 5 — Stay Organised

Everything stays linked to the relevant matter, so you can see what is coming up and what still needs attention.

Who Uses CasePath?

Self-Represented Litigants

People managing their own family law or protection order matter — and those working with legal representation — who need to stay organised, prepare documents, track deadlines, and understand the process. CasePath supports both pathways: whether you are representing yourself or preparing materials to share with your lawyer.

People Preparing For Mediation

Those who want to arrive at mediation with clear notes, issues identified and documents ready to discuss. Preparation tools help you organise what matters before you sit down at the table.

Parents Managing Ongoing Matters

Parents with existing orders who need to track dates, communications and children’s arrangements over time. Your Case keeps everything connected to your matter as circumstances change.

People Working Alongside Lawyers

Clients who use CasePath to prepare materials and stay organised, then share information with their lawyer. You organise facts and materials; your lawyer can assess the law, risk, and court strategy.
