About the Kids

Children usually do best when adult conflict is kept away from them, routines stay predictable, and both parents support safe, appropriate relationships wherever the law and the facts allow.

They should not carry messages between parents, pick sides, or worry that loving one parent will hurt the other. Simple, repeated reassurance that it is okay to love both parents reduces loyalty conflict and protects their wellbeing.
