Privacy

How CasePath UK currently handles account identity, UK preparation information, Contact, and Feedback.

At a glance: Your CasePath sign-in is a global account. Signed-in UK preparation is stored in the United Kingdom. Contact and Feedback are sent by email, not stored in a UK application database.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Overview

This page describes current CasePath UK practices. It is not legal advice about your rights.

CasePath UK is a family-court preparation product for England and Wales. People use it to organise information, complete preparation assistants and checklists, and get ready to work with official forms.

It is not a social network. It is not designed for staff to routinely read your preparation answers.

2. How the service is structured

CasePath UK uses two separate parts:

  • Your CasePath account — the email and password you use to sign in. That identity is the same account used on casepath.com.au. The account service is not the UK preparation database.
  • Your UK preparation — the answers, case details, assistants, and checklists you save while using this UK product. When you are signed in, that material is stored in a United Kingdom data service hosted in London. It is not written into the Australian CasePath application database.

In everyday language: signing in tells CasePath who you are. The UK product then keeps your England and Wales preparation in the UK. The two are linked by your account, but the case answers themselves stay on the UK side.

3. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the product, this may include:

  • your name and email when you send a Contact or Feedback message
  • account email and password when you sign in or create an account
  • preparation information you type into case fields, assistants, and checklists — for example names, dates, court details, and notes you choose to enter
  • a last-visited location used to resume your work

Do not enter more personal information than you need. Do not use a shared device for sensitive answers.

4. Account and identity information

The CasePath account holds the email address and authentication details needed to sign you in. The UK product records that your account has used CasePath UK, together with the time you first used it and the time you were last active.

Your email address is not copied into a UK case-data table. The UK participation record is an account identifier and timestamps only.

5. Preparation information

When you are signed in, saved UK preparation is sent to the UK data service and also kept as a copy in this browser so the page can work quickly.

When you are not signed in, preparation stays in this browser only (local storage). It is not uploaded.

Signed-in preparation can include the shared case fields, assistant answers, checklist ticks and notes, and a resume location. Help-request notes you save on this device stay in the browser and are not uploaded with that cloud envelope.

6. Contact submissions

The Contact form collects name, email, subject, and message. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to reduce automated abuse.

The message is delivered by email to [email protected] through Resend. CasePath UK does not write Contact submissions into a UK application database.

The email also includes the page address, the route you were on, whether you were signed in, a user identifier if you were signed in, the time received, and your browser’s user-agent string. Do not include case documents, passwords, or detailed information about children or proceedings.

7. Feedback submissions

The Feedback button on UK pages collects a category, optional email, and a message. Turnstile is used in the same way as Contact.

Feedback is also delivered by email to [email protected] through Resend. It is not stored in a UK application database and does not attach your saved preparation.

The same kind of page, route, sign-in, and browser metadata is included so we can understand where a problem happened.

8. Technical and security metadata

To operate sign-in, Contact, Feedback, and saved work, the product processes:

  • browser and device information sent with ordinary web requests
  • Turnstile challenge tokens
  • sign-in session details stored by the account service in this browser
  • timestamps for UK participation and saved-work updates

9. Authentication

Sign-in uses the global CasePath account service. The UK product checks that your sign-in token is valid before it reads or writes UK saved work.

Cloudflare Turnstile is used on sign-in, create-account, and password-recovery, as well as Contact and Feedback.

This UK version does not load Australian billing, vault, or multi-factor-authentication product features.

10. Where information is stored

  • Signed-in UK preparation — United Kingdom (London) data service.
  • Signed-out preparation — this browser only.
  • Account identity — the global CasePath account service, which is separate from the UK preparation store and is not hosted in that London data service.
  • Contact — emailed to [email protected]; not stored as UK application records.
  • Feedback — emailed to [email protected]; not stored as UK application records.

London hosting of UK preparation does not, by itself, mean the service is legally compliant. That assessment still requires specialist review.

11. Organisations that help operate the service

Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • The CasePath account service — sign-in, password reset, and account email
  • The UK hosting provider (Supabase) — the London data service for signed-in UK preparation
  • Cloudflare — Turnstile bot protection
  • Resend — sending Contact and Feedback email
  • Google — loading fonts used on these pages
  • Plausible Analytics — privacy-focused usage analytics
  • Stripe — payment processing when you use UK checkout or the billing portal

When you start checkout from a published UK plan, payment information is processed by Stripe. CasePath UK does not store full payment card numbers on its own systems. It does not currently send preparation information to an AI provider.

12. Analytics, cookies, and local storage

These UK pages embed Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused usage measurement tool. They do not embed Google Analytics.

The UK product does not set its own application cookies. Sign-in keeps a session in this browser’s local storage through the account service. Preparation keys in this browser use a casepath_uk_ prefix. A short-lived session value is also used for glossary search and password-recovery return.

The hosting environment may inject its own technical measurements. That is outside the UK application code in this repository and is not described here as a CasePath UK analytics product.

13. Security

Signed-in UK preparation is reached through a server function that checks your CasePath sign-in token. Direct anonymous access to UK saved-work tables is denied.

Connections use ordinary encrypted HTTPS. No online service can promise absolute security. Protect your password and device, and be careful what you type.

This page does not publish internal security controls, project identifiers, or access keys.

14. Retention

While your UK saved work exists, it remains available so you can continue preparation. If you delete UK saved work, the preparation payload is removed from the UK data service and from this browser’s saved-work keys. A record that your account used CasePath UK can remain.

Contact and Feedback exist as email messages in the destination mailbox and in the email provider’s systems.

LEGAL/PRIVACY REVIEW REQUIRED

Fixed retention periods, backup lifetime, and mailbox retention have not been approved. This page does not invent those periods.

15. Deletion and controls you have today

You can:

  • edit preparation while using the product
  • sign out (this does not delete saved UK work)
  • delete UK saved work from Account
  • ask a question through Contact

Deleting UK saved work does not delete your CasePath account and does not delete Australian work. Whole-account deletion is not available in this UK version.

  • Turnstile and font loading involve Cloudflare and Google services that operate internationally

18. Information about children

Family-court preparation often includes information about children. Enter only what you need. CasePath UK is not an emergency service.

If you or a child are in immediate danger, call 999. The National Domestic Abuse Helpline is 0808 2000 247.
