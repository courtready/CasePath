2. What CasePath UK is

CasePath UK is an educational preparation product for England and Wales only. It does not cover Scotland or Northern Ireland.

It can help you:

  • understand common family-court pathways in plain English
  • organise information about a matter
  • work through preparation assistants and checklists
  • prepare material that you can check against official HMCTS forms

CasePath is not the court, not HMCTS, and not a government service. Official GOV.UK and HMCTS sources remain authoritative.

3. Information only — not legal advice

CasePath UK provides general information and self-help tools. Nothing on this website is legal advice, a legal opinion, legal representation, or a legal service.

You should obtain independent advice from a qualified family solicitor, a regulated legal adviser, or a local advice service before relying on information or documents you prepare here.

4. No solicitor-client relationship

Using CasePath UK does not create a solicitor-client relationship, or any other professional retainer, between you and the people who operate, develop, or contribute to CasePath.

5. Official sources and forms

Court rules, forms and fees change. You are responsible for checking current official sources, including GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk.

Where CasePath helps you prepare material for an official form, the official HMCTS form remains the authoritative document. CasePath does not guarantee that a court or tribunal will accept anything you prepare or file.

6. Your responsibility

You are responsible for:
