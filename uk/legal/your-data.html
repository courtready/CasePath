Your Data

A plain-English picture of what CasePath UK knows, where it lives, and what you can control.

At a glance: Your sign-in is a CasePath account. Your England and Wales preparation is stored in the UK when you are signed in. Administrators can see that you used the UK product. They cannot open your saved answers through the UK admin tools.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. What this page is for

Family-court information is personal. This page explains the current UK product in ordinary language. It is not a marketing page and not legal advice. The formal description is on Privacy.

2. What CasePath knows about you

If you only browse, the product keeps preparation in this browser when you type it, and it does not upload those answers.

If you sign in, CasePath also knows:

  • the email address on your CasePath account
  • that this account has used the UK product, and roughly when
  • the UK preparation you choose to save

If you send Contact or Feedback, the people who read that mailbox see the message you typed plus the page you were on.

3. What UK preparation is stored

Saved UK work can include:

  • shared case fields you complete
  • answers inside preparation assistants
  • checklist ticks and notes
  • where you last were in the product, so you can resume

That is your working material for England and Wales. It is not an Australian case file.

4. Where substantive UK data lives

When you are signed in, the preparation described above is stored in a United Kingdom data service in London. It is not written into the Australian CasePath application database.

When you are signed out, it stays on this device.

Your email and password live with the global CasePath account, not in that London preparation store.

5. Account identity versus UK application data

Think of two folders:

  • Account folder — who you are (email, sign-in). The same folder works on casepath.com.au and on this UK site.
  • UK work folder — what you typed for England and Wales. That folder is only in the UK product (and in this browser as a working copy).

Signing in on the UK site does not load Australian case files. Signing in on the Australian site does not load UK preparation.

6. What administrators can and cannot see

UK administrators can see that an account has used CasePath UK, the participation dates, whether saved work exists, and — when that lookup is configured — the account email.

UK administrators cannot see your saved preparation answers through the UK admin tools. Those tools are built so they do not return the preparation payload. They also do not show Australian cases or Australian vault files. This page does not describe Stripe payment records.

Limited operational access could still be needed for security, abuse, legal obligations, or support you request. That is not the same as staff routinely reading your answers.

7. Contact and Feedback

Contact messages are emailed to [email protected]. Feedback messages are emailed to [email protected]. They are not saved as UK database rows. They do not include your preparation file. They do include the page you were on and whether you were signed in.

Write only what is needed. The form asks you not to send case documents or details about children or proceedings.

8. What you can control today

  • Edit your answers while you use the product.
  • Sign out — stops this browser acting as you. It does not wipe UK saved work.
  • Delete UK saved work on Account — removes the UK preparation payload from the UK store and from this browser’s saved-work keys. It does not delete the CasePath account.
  • Contact us if you need help understanding what is held.

Whole-account deletion is not available in this UK version.

9. What happens when you sign out

The product stops using your account for cloud saves. UK work already saved in London stays there until you delete it. A copy may still be in this browser until you delete UK saved work or clear the browser’s site data.

Quick Exit leaves the site and does not itself delete your cloud-saved UK work.

10. Browser storage

This browser may hold:

  • your UK preparation keys (so the pages work when you are signed out, and as a cache when you are signed in)
  • your CasePath sign-in session
  • small session values for glossary search and returning from a password-reset email

Clearing site data in the browser removes the local copy. It does not by itself delete signed-in work stored in London — use Account for that.

11. Other organisations involved

Sign-in, London hosting, Turnstile, Resend email, Google Fonts, and Stripe payment processing are described on Privacy. CasePath UK does not currently send preparation information to an AI provider.

12. Honesty about gaps

Backup lifetime, a finished UK GDPR notice, and whole-account deletion are not established for this UK version. Those items are listed as review items on the Privacy page rather than guessed here.
