UK subscription checkout uses Stripe. Approved UK refund wording is still under legal review.

At a glance: The UK pricing page can start Stripe Checkout for published individual subscriptions. Approved UK refund, cancellation, and Consumer Rights Act wording has not been published.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. How UK payments are taken

Published UK individual subscription options can start Stripe Checkout from the pricing page. Card details are handled by Stripe. A Stripe customer billing portal can be opened to manage an existing recurring subscription.

If you have not paid CasePath for a UK service, there is nothing to refund for a UK purchase.

2. Australian charges

If you have a paid Australian CasePath account, that is a separate product. Questions about an Australian charge should be raised through the Australian site’s Contact and refund pages, not treated as a UK payment.

3. Refund and cancellation wording

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

No approved UK commercial refund, cancellation, or Consumer Rights Act wording exists yet. This page will not invent cooling-off periods, goodwill rules, or statutory refund rights. Until that review is complete, billing questions can be sent through Contact with the date, amount, and the email on the account.

4. Questions

If something on a statement looks like a CasePath charge and you are unsure which product it relates to, use Contact and include the date, amount, and the email on the account.
