Pricing

England & Wales pricing for individuals and law firms. Amounts are in pounds sterling.

Individual: £19.99 every 1 month, £49 every 3 months, £89 every 6 months, and £149 every 12 months (all recurring). Law firm seats start at £480 per seat / year, with a £600 per firm / year minimum for solo and small firms. 10–49 seats are £650 per seat / year.
