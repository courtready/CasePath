How CasePath Works

Built for people — not lawyers

You shouldn't need to speak lawyer to get started.

Family court terminology can be overwhelming. CasePath starts with ordinary questions:

“What's happening?”

“What are you trying to do?”

“Is there already a court order?”

rather than expecting you to understand terms like:

C100 C1A FL401 C79 C2 Form A D8

CasePath introduces the terminology when it becomes useful.

General information

What CasePath is — and is not

CasePath provides general information and document-preparation assistance. It is not a law firm. It does not provide legal advice. It does not replace a solicitor.

You remain responsible for checking information and documents before filing. Official HMCTS forms remain authoritative.

This product is for England and Wales only. It does not cover Scotland or Northern Ireland.
