How CasePath Works

Family court forms and jargon, turned into plain English — step by step, at your own pace.

CasePath helps you understand and prepare for family court. It is not legal advice, and does not replace a solicitor. For advice about your specific situation, speak to a qualified family solicitor or a local legal advice service.

A Simple Process

Most people arrive at CasePath feeling overwhelmed by forms, deadlines and legal language. Here's how CasePath helps, from start to finish.

Tell Us What's Going On

Choose the situation closest to yours — arranging to see your children, applying for protection, sorting out finances, or starting a divorce.

2

Answer Simple Questions

CasePath asks you plain-English questions one at a time, instead of handing you a whole form and a wall of legal wording at once.

Who Uses CasePath?

CasePath is built for anyone navigating family court alone — not just one type of person or one side of a case.

Self-Represented Litigants

People managing their own family court case without a solicitor, most often because legal representation isn't affordable.

People With Some Legal Help

Those working with a solicitor for the harder parts, who want to prepare routine forms themselves to save on cost.

Anyone Feeling Lost in the Process

Even if you're not filling in a form today, the glossary is free to explore any time you just want to understand a term.

Responding to an Application

People who've been served papers and need to understand what's being asked of them and how to respond — not just those starting a case.
